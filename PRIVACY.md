# BingTranslatorX Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026.

This policy describes how the BingTranslatorX browser extension handles data in Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome.

## Bing Dictionary lookups

When a hover or selection triggers a dictionary lookup, BingTranslatorX reads the lookup text from the page and sends it directly to Microsoft Bing Dictionary over HTTPS to retrieve definitions. Hover lookups are enabled by default on a new installation. When pronunciation audio is available, the browser may also request it from Bing. Opening a dictionary result takes you to Bing.

Microsoft receives the lookup text and standard request information such as your IP address. Microsoft handles that data under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/privacystatement). The extension uses lookup text to provide dictionary results. BingTranslatorX keeps no lookup history and has no developer-operated server that receives your lookups.

## Optional on-device translation

If you enable **Text translation** on a supported browser, longer selections are translated on your device using the browser's built-in Translator API. The browser may download the required language pack. The extension processes the selected text locally for this feature.

## Settings and your choices

BingTranslatorX stores preferences locally in your browser, including whether the extension is enabled, hover and shortcut settings, the language filter, the popup color, and your text translation preference. It also remembers whether it has shown the text translation setup screen.

Open BingTranslatorX from the browser toolbar to view or change these settings. Turn off **Translate when hovering on word** to stop hover-triggered requests. Word selections can still trigger Bing lookups. To stop all lookups, turn off the main **Enable** option or disable the extension in your browser. Turn off **Text translation** to stop on-device translation of longer selections. Uninstalling the extension removes its locally stored preferences.
